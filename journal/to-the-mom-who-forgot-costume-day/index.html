To the Mom Who Forgot It Was Costume Day

For the morning the school WhatsApp group reminds you, at 7.12am.

7.12am. The school group lights up. Photos of tiny astronauts, princesses, one very committed dinosaur.

Costume day. Today.

Your kid is standing in the doorway in regular uniform, shoes on the wrong feet, completely unaware that his life is about to be ruined.

Breathe.

The 10-minute rescue

  • A plain t-shirt plus a drawn-on logo is a superhero.
  • A dressing gown and a stick is a wizard.
  • A cardboard box is a robot, a car or a Minecraft block. Every time.
  • Face paint, or a marker in a pinch, carries most of it.

And if none of that works? He goes in regular clothes. He survives. Someone else forgot too. Probably three someones.

Here's what he'll remember: that you made him laugh in the car about the cardboard box. Not that you missed a message in a group of 87 parents.

You're doing fine. Better than fine. Now go drink your coffee.
