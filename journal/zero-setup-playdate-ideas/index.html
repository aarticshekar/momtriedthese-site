7 Playdate Ideas That Need Zero Setup

For when the kids arrive in ten minutes and you have nothing planned.

The doorbell's going to ring in ten minutes and you have planned exactly nothing. Same.

These need no shopping and almost no cleanup.

1. Fort building

Every cushion, every blanket, two chairs. They'll build for an hour and play inside it for another.

2. A dance-off

One phone, one speaker, three songs each. Freeze when the music stops.

3. The build challenge

"You have 15 minutes to build the tallest tower." Use whatever's around. LEGO, magnetic tiles, books, cups.

4. A treasure hunt

Hide five things. Write five silly clues. Done.

5. Story dice, without dice

One kid starts a story with one sentence. Next kid adds one. It goes somewhere ridiculous by round three.

6. A card game

UNO or Spot It works across ages and needs no explaining.

7. Kitchen helpers

Let them make their own snack. Spread, sprinkle, stack. They'll eat what they made.

The secret to a good playdate is not the activity. It's starting it before the "I'm bored" starts.
