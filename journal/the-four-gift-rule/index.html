The Four-Gift Rule: Want, Need, Read, Do

A simple way to stop the Christmas list getting out of hand.

Links on this page go to Amazon. If you buy, we may earn a small commission at no cost to you.

Something they want. Something they need. Something to read. Something to do.

Four gifts. That's the rule.

It isn't about spending less. It's about every gift having a job, so nothing gets lost under the pile.

Want

The thing they asked for. The one on the list in their handwriting.

Need

Something they'll use every day. School gear, a good bottle, the lunchbox that doesn't leak. Boring to buy, loved to use.

Read

A book in a series they already love, so it gets finished.

Do

Something to do together, or something to make. This is the one they remember.
