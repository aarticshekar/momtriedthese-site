What to Keep Stocked for the Day Help Doesn't Show Up

A working mom's backup plan for the morning the cook or nanny cancels.

Links on this page go to Amazon. If you buy, we may earn a small commission at no cost to you.

The message comes in at 7am. "Madam, not coming today." You have a presentation at 10.

Fun, right? Wrong.

Treat it like any other risk at work. It will happen. The only question is whether you've planned for it.

The freezer drawer

Cook double once a week and freeze single portions. Dal, rajma, pasta sauce, a curry. On a bad day, dinner is a reheat, not a decision.

The pantry shelf

  • Ready-to-eat parathas or rotis.
  • Instant poha or upma mix.
  • Peanut butter, crackers, cheese slices.
  • A box of good cereal that only comes out on these days.

The kid box

A small box they only see on help-free days. A new colouring book, stickers, a puzzle. Novelty is the whole trick. It buys you an hour.

The mom bit

Water within reach. Coffee you actually like. And permission to order in, without guilt.

You can't stop the cancellation. You can make it a slightly annoying day instead of a disaster.
