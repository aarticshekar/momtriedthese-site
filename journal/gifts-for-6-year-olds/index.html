Six is the age where they want to do it themselves. Not watch you do it. Not help. Do it.

So this list leans into that. Every gift here is something a six year old can start and finish without an adult hovering, which is the real gift, for both of you.

A first set they can finish alone, and one toy that grows with them for years.
