Why We Bought the Smiggle Minecraft Lunchbox

Removable dividers, a design a Minecraft fan is proud to carry, and what to know before you buy.

Links on this page go to Amazon. If you buy, we may earn a small commission at no cost to you.

Minecraft is the current obsession in our house. So when the lunchbox needed replacing, there was only ever going to be one design.

But I wouldn't have bought it on looks alone.

What sold it

The dividers come out. Some days it's one big sandwich. Some days it's crackers, fruit, cheese and a little something, all in separate corners. One box handles both.

What to know before you buy

  • It's a bento-style box, not a thermal one. Hot food won't stay hot.
  • Smiggle designs rotate. If your kid wants a specific one, don't wait.
  • Hand-wash the lid to keep the print looking good.

Who it suits

Kids who like their food separated, and anyone who'd rather carry a lunchbox they're proud of. A box they like tends to come home emptier.
