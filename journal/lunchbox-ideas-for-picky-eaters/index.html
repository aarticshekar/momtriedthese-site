A Week of Lunchbox Ideas for Picky Eaters

Five school lunches that come home eaten, for the kid who eats four foods.

Links on this page go to Amazon. If you buy, we may earn a small commission at no cost to you.

If your kid eats four foods and three of them are beige, hello. Welcome.

The trick I've found isn't hiding vegetables. It's making lunch small, familiar and a bit fun.

Monday: pinwheels

A roti or wrap, a thin layer of cheese spread, rolled tight, cut into rounds. They look like a treat.

Tuesday: mini paniyaram

Leftover dosa batter in a paniyaram pan. Little crispy balls. Pack a tiny pot of chutney or ketchup.

Wednesday: pasta salad

Their usual pasta, served cold with sweetcorn and a bit of cheese. Same food, new outfit.

Thursday: build-your-own crackers

Crackers, cheese cubes, cucumber rounds, grapes. They assemble. Assembling is half the eating.

Friday: breakfast for lunch

Mini uttapam or idli with a dip. Nobody complains on a Friday.

Two rules that help

  • One safe food in every box, always.
  • One new thing, tiny portion, no pressure.

A box with compartments helps more than you'd think. Foods that don't touch are a big deal at this age.
