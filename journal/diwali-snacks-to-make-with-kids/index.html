Easy Diwali Snacks to Make With Kids

Simple festive snacks little hands can actually help with.

Making something together is half of what makes it a festival. The other half is eating it before it's finished.

These are simple enough that kids can really help, not just watch.

Coconut ladoo

Desiccated coconut, condensed milk, a little cardamom. Mix, roll into balls, roll in more coconut. No stove for the kids' part.

Chocolate-dipped Marie biscuits

Melt chocolate, dip half the biscuit, add sprinkles. Very festive, very messy, very worth it.

Poha chivda

Roast thin poha, peanuts and curry leaves with a little oil and turmeric. Kids can do the mixing at the end.

Fruit "diyas"

Half an orange, scooped out, a small tea light inside (lit by you). Pretty on the table, and they'll want to make ten.

Don't aim for perfect. Aim for flour on the floor and a story to tell.
