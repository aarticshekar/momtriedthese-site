A water bottle that also sprays a cool mist. Here's why, and what to know.

It's a water bottle. It's also a mister. Press a button and it sprays a fine, cool mist.

That's why it made our list.

Why it makes sense

Hot days, outdoor play, sports day, the walk from the car to the school gate. A quick spray to the face cools you down far faster than a sip alone.

What to know before you buy

  • The spray uses the same water you drink. Fill it with cold water for the full effect.
  • Teach them the spray is for faces, not friends. Early.
  • Wash the spray nozzle now and then, it's the bit that gets ignored.

Who it suits

Kids in warm places, kids who play outside, and kids who need a reason to carry their bottle at all.
