The Ten Minutes That Are Yours

Not a self-care routine. Just ten minutes, protected like a meeting.

Everyone keeps telling moms to do self-care, as if we're sitting around with spare hours and simply forgot.

You don't have an hour. Most days you don't have twenty minutes.

You might have ten. After drop-off. Or at 9.40pm, when the house finally goes quiet and the dishwasher is humming.

So protect those ten minutes like a meeting with someone important. Phone face down. No "quickly" replying to anything.

What fits in ten minutes

  • A cup of something hot, drunk while it's still hot. Revolutionary, I know.
  • A spritz of rose water and a window open.
  • Three pages of a book that has nothing to do with parenting or work.
  • Absolutely nothing. Staring at the wall counts.

It sounds small. It is small.

But it's the difference between a day that happened to you and a day that had a little bit of you in it.
