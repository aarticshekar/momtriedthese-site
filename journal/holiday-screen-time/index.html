Some Screen Time Over the Holidays Is Not a Failure

For the mom feeling guilty about a film on the sofa.

The holidays are long. Longer than you remember them being.

At some point, there will be a film on a slow afternoon. Maybe two. Maybe a whole morning of a show you've now heard so many times you know the songs.

That's not a parenting failure. That's a tradition.

The balance that works

One thing off the screen every day. A walk, a game, a craft, a book before bed. That's it.

Do that, and the rest is fine.

Nobody is grading you. And if they are, they're not your people.
