Why We Bought a Cast Iron Paniyaram Pan

Leftover batter becomes a new breakfast. What it does and what to know.

Links on this page go to Amazon. If you buy, we may earn a small commission at no cost to you.

If dosa batter is a weekly thing in your kitchen, you know the last bit. The bottom of the box, not enough for a full round of dosas.

That's what the paniyaram pan is for.

What it does

The pan has little round hollows. A spoon of batter in each, a few drops of oil, turn them once. Out come crispy little bites, soft inside.

Add chopped onion, coriander and a pinch of chilli for grown-ups. Leave it plain for kids.

What to know before you buy

  • Cast iron needs seasoning before first use, and a wipe of oil after washing. It rewards you by getting more non-stick over time.
  • It's heavy. That's the point, it holds heat evenly.
  • Use a wooden skewer or a small spoon to turn them.

Who it suits

South Indian kitchens, obviously. But also anyone who wants a breakfast that fits in a lunchbox.
