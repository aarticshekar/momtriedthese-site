What to Stock for the Day Help Doesn't Show Up

The cook calls in sick. The nanny has an emergency. School shuts and I have a presentation in 90 minutes. Fun, right? Wrong. So I run the house like a project with a risk register. This is the contingency plan.

Links go to Amazon. I may earn a small commission at no cost to you. Details

The rule: decide on the good day, not the bad one

On the bad day you have no time and no patience. So every decision gets made earlier: what goes in the freezer, what sits on the shelf, what the kids reach for. Think of it like a spare tire. Nobody admires it until the day it saves the trip.
