# Mom Tried These: voice guide

MTT is an independent brand. Take the voice from Aarti's own writing, leave the personal story behind.

## Take
- Short lines, lots of white space. Written like a text to a friend.
- Asides and little exclamations: "Ugh!", "Gosh!", "Sorry!", "mind you!"
- Tag questions and quick turns: "no?", "right? Wrong."
- Work language turned on home life: SLA, stakeholders, contingency plan.
- Self-deprecating and honest. Admit the messy bit, then the fix. A wink at the end when it fits.

## Leave behind
- No biography: no family members by role or name, no backstory, no workplace details, no links to Substack or aarti.blog.
- Never name the child or state an age for him. "The kid" at most.

## Rules
- No em dashes. Ever.
- Never invent an experience. Tags: "Bought by us" only for items in data/bought list; "On my list" for site list items; "Researched pick" for everything else.
- No AI personas, no fake faces or voices, no testimonials.
- No Amazon product images in videos or pins. Type and brand colour only, or Aarti's own photos.
- Captions end with: "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases."
- Avoid AI tells: "Here's the thing", "game changer", "Win-win", "Whether you're", "dive in", "elevate", "seamless", "journey", habitual triple lists.
