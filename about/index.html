About

Hi. I'm a working mom who researches everything like it's a big decision. Even the lunchbox. Especially the lunchbox.

Mom Tried These started as a list I kept for myself. What actually worked, what didn't, and what I'd tell a friend to buy if she asked me at the school gate.

There's a lot of noise out there. Hundred-item gift guides, "must-haves" that nobody needs, and the same ten products dressed up in different lists. This is the opposite. Fewer things, each with a reason.

Two labels, always

We bought this means exactly that. We paid for it, it lives in our house.
